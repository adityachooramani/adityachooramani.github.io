Dustinel AI

Lead full-stack developer, team of 3, Mar 2026
CloudAI/ML

A worker-safety platform with real-time dashboards over WebSockets and role-based access. The frontend is React and Next.js, the backend is Node and Express, and hazard assessment runs on GPT-4o and Azure AI Vision. All 18 Azure services are provisioned with infrastructure-as-code, sign-in goes through Entra External ID, and there are no credentials in the codebase.

50+active users
18Azure services via IaC
<100mstoken validation
$25kAzure credits awarded
How a check-in moves through the system
  1. Worker appNext.js PWA. Workers sign in with a phone OTP and send a selfie and a short video.
  2. API ManagementChecks the JWT on every request before anything reaches the backend.
  3. App ServiceNode 22. Every secret is pulled from Key Vault through a Managed Identity.
  4. AI pipelineAI Vision checks for helmets and masks, Cognitive Search finds the right safety documents, GPT-4o writes the guidance, and Translator and Speech deliver it in the worker's language.
  5. Service BusHigh-risk results go out as an SMS to the manager and a live alert on the dashboard.
Next.js · Node.js · PostgreSQL · Docker · Azure · GPT-4o

About

Aditya Chooramani
Mathura, India

I've been hooked on computers since I was six, back when they were mostly for games. I got my own in 4th standard and spent hours clicking every button and opening every setting to see what it did. That went fine until the day I deleted the C drive to free up some space. It worked, technically.

Around then I found out hackers were a thing, and I decided I'd be one. The legal kind, the one people call to find the holes before someone else does. BCA was the natural way to study computers properly, and I graduate in 2027.

Now I build things on AWS and Azure, try to break into them, and write about how it went on dev.to. Away from the keyboard I play chess, watch movies and series, and travel with a camera.
