Projects

Dustinel AI

2026 · cloud, AI/ML · lead full-stack developer, team of 3

A worker-safety app we built for Microsoft Imagine Cup. Before a shift, workers send a selfie and a short video. Azure AI Vision checks for helmets and masks, GPT-4o writes safety guidance, and high-risk results alert the site manager by SMS. The frontend is Next.js, the backend is Node and Express, and all 18 Azure services are provisioned as infrastructure-as-code with no credentials in the codebase. It has 50+ active users, validates tokens in under 100ms, and we were awarded $25k in Azure credits.

How a check-in works
  1. The worker signs in with a phone OTP and sends a selfie and a short video from the Next.js app.
  2. API Management checks the JWT on every request before anything reaches the backend.
  3. App Service (Node 22) pulls every secret from Key Vault through a Managed Identity.
  4. AI Vision checks for helmets and masks, Cognitive Search finds the right safety documents, GPT-4o writes the guidance, and Translator and Speech deliver it in the worker's language.
  5. High-risk results go through Service Bus as an SMS to the manager and a live alert on the dashboard.
Next.js, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Docker, Azure, GPT-4o

Sentinel Eye

Feb 2026 · cloud · lead backend and cloud developer, team of 3

A geospatial analytics backend on AWS, built over two days at the Hyperspace Innovation Hackathon in Ghaziabad. It serves REST endpoints over MySQL, ingests 1.6 GB of geospatial data from S3, responds in under 200ms, and sends SNS alerts when infrastructure changes. IAM permissions were kept to the minimum from the first commit, and API delivery came out 40% faster.

Node.js, Express, MySQL, AWS S3, RDS, SNS, IAM, Docker

Sagar Sahayak

Dec 2025 · AI/ML, backend · team of 4

A maritime monitoring system for the national security track of Smart India Hackathon. I wrote the Django backend, which takes in high-frequency sensor data and turns raw hardware payloads into REST responses. We also trained and fine-tuned PyTorch models for underwater threat detection and image enhancement, then optimized them with ONNX and OpenVINO. It held 99% uptime through field testing and took our team to the national finals, in the top 2% of entries.

About

I've been hooked on computers since I was six, back when they were mostly for games. I got my own in 4th standard and spent hours clicking every button and opening every setting to see what it did. That went fine until the day I deleted the C drive to free up some space. It worked, technically.

Around then I found out hackers were a thing, and I decided I'd be one. The legal kind, the one people call to find the holes before someone else does. BCA was the natural way to study computers properly, and I graduate in 2027.
