Projects

Dustinel AI

2026 · cloud, AI/ML · lead full-stack developer, team of 3

A worker-safety app we built for Microsoft Imagine Cup. Before a shift, workers send a selfie and a short video. Azure AI Vision checks for helmets and masks, GPT-4o writes safety guidance, and high-risk results alert the site manager by SMS. The frontend is Next.js, the backend is Node and Express, and all 18 Azure services are provisioned as infrastructure-as-code with no credentials in the codebase. It has 50+ active users, validates tokens in under 100ms, and we were awarded $25k in Azure credits.

How a check-in works
  1. The worker signs in with a phone OTP and sends a selfie and a short video from the Next.js app.
  2. API Management checks the JWT on every request before anything reaches the backend.
  3. App Service (Node 22) pulls every secret from Key Vault through a Managed Identity.
  4. AI Vision checks for helmets and masks, Cognitive Search finds the right safety documents, GPT-4o writes the guidance, and Translator and Speech deliver it in the worker's language.
  5. High-risk results go through Service Bus as an SMS to the manager and a live alert on the dashboard.
Next.js, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Docker, Azure, GPT-4o
